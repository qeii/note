How to use these cards

Pick one card during your weekly check-in or four cards during your Monthly Money Date. There are no right or wrong answers. The goal is to understand your partner's "Money Why" and build a shared vision that excites you both.

💡 How many cards per date? Use 3–5 cards per money date — enough to spark real conversation without rushing. One card from each category keeps it balanced. Save the rest for next month.
