HOW TO USE THIS TRACKERCouples Wealth Tracker2
NEW TO OURSTEADY?  To get the most from this product, read the companion How-To Guide first — it walks through every section step-by-step. How-To Guide
This tracker is designed for your monthly Money Date — a recurring 30–60 min session where you review finances together. Print each monthly sheet, or fill digitally. Consistency beats perfection. Even 20 minutes a month changes everything.
4-STEP SYSTEM
Step 1 · Start of Year
Fill in Annual Dashboard (p.3)
Set your 3 big goals
Record starting net worth
List all accounts + balances
Step 2 · Each Month
Complete Monthly Budget Sheet
Update goal progress bars
Log debt payoff progress
Note one win + one lesson
Step 3 · Mid-Year
Review Annual Dashboard
Adjust goals if life changed
Celebrate progress
Check investment allocations
Step 4 · End of Year
Complete Year-in-Review (p.19)
Calculate annual savings rate
Final vs. starting net worth
Set next year's intentions

TIPS FOR SUCCESS
Schedule it
Same day each month. Put it in the calendar like a real date.
No blame zone
Curiosity over criticism. You're on the same team.
Celebrate wins
Even small ones. Paid $50 extra on debt? That counts.
Start anywhere
Behind on tracking? Start from today. Imperfect beats never.
🖨 To print: Open in your browser → File → Print → All Pages → Save as PDF or send to printer.
💻 To fill digitally: Open in Adobe Reader (free at adobe.com) or Preview (Mac). Fill the fields and save as a new file with your names — e.g. "Smith-Jones Tracker 2025.pdf". Your data saves with the file.
Annual Dashboard totals don't auto-calculate — add your year-end numbers manually in the Year-in-Review on page 19. That page is designed to be your complete year-end summary.
