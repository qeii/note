HOW TO USE THIS KITCouples Financial Reset Kit — The 90-Day Reboot2
NEW TO OURSTEADY?  To get the most from this product, read the companion How-To Guide first — it walks through every section step-by-step. How-To Guide
This kit is a 90-day step-by-step action plan — not a workbook you fill in once and forget. It's designed to be picked up weekly, for 13 short sessions, with three monthly milestone reviews. Each session takes 20–40 minutes. You don't need to be financial experts. You just need to show up, together, once a week.
THE 3-PHASE STRUCTURE
1
CLARITY · Weeks 1–4
Stop guessing. See the full picture — where your money actually goes, what you actually owe, and what a real fresh start looks like for your specific situation.
2
STRUCTURE · Weeks 5–8
Build the system. Automate what can be automated. Design your budget to fit your life — not some template. Eliminate the decisions that keep tripping you up.
3
MOMENTUM · Weeks 9–13
Accelerate. With clarity and structure in place, direct your surplus toward your first big goal. Lock in habits that will outlast the 90 days.
HOW EACH WEEK WORKS
BEFORE
Skim that week's action items — 2 minutes, before your session.
DURING
Sit together (phones away), work through the tasks and the check-in questions. Write actual answers — not plans to think about later.
AFTER
Do the one action item you committed to before your next session. Not three. One.
MONTHLY
Complete the Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90 check-in sheets. These are the pivot points where you assess, adjust, and recommit.
GROUND RULES FOR THE 90 DAYS
No blame for the past. Whatever the numbers say at the starting point, you're here now, together. The reset starts from today.
Both partners show up. One person running the finances while the other checks out is how you got here. The reboot requires two people.
Progress over perfection. A week where you did 3 out of 5 tasks is a good week. Don't let perfect be the enemy of better.
Write it down. Decisions that aren't written down don't exist. Every commitment goes on paper (or in the field).
The 90 days are non-negotiable. The method is flexible. If a task doesn't fit your situation, adapt it — but don't skip the week.

Weeks 5–8
WEEK 5
Design Your Reset Budget
Focus: Build a budget you'll actually live by.
Choose your budget framework — then fill in your numbers. Use your Phase 1 data as the starting point.
50/30/20 Rule
50% needs · 30% wants · 20% savings + debt. Simple and intuitive. Best for couples new to budgeting.
Pay Yourself First
Save + invest first, spend the rest freely. Best for couples who struggle to save from what's left.
Zero-Based
Every dollar assigned a job. Income minus all allocations = $0. Best for couples with variable spend or tight margins.

Pick Your Priority: Debt or Goal?
Focus: Direct your surplus with intention.
Now that you have a surplus, the question is: where does it go first? Use the priority decision below.
THE PRIORITY LADDER — work down from the top
1. Emergency fund fully funded (3 months expenses) — if not, this comes first.
2. Employer-matched retirement (up to the match) — it's a guaranteed 50–100% return.
3. High-interest debt (above 7%) — pay this aggressively before investing further.
4. First shared goal savings — direct your monthly surplus here.
5. Medium-rate debt (3–7%) and additional investing — split between both.
