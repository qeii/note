The Money Date FrameworkThe Couples Wealth Accelerator4
The 45-Minute Structure: Every monthly Money Date follows the same 5-part rhythm. This removes decision fatigue and keeps you from wandering into argument territory.
The 5-Part Money Date Rhythm
1
Warm-Up
5 min
Check in emotionally. How are you both feeling about money?
2
Review
10 min
Last month's numbers. Income, expenses, savings, debt.
3
Priorities
10 min
This month's focus. One financial + one relationship priority.
4
Actions
10 min
Specific commitments. Who does what by when.
5
Close
10 min
Reflect & connect. One win, one gratitude, one excitement.
Money Date Rules
Same time, same place. Consistency beats intensity.
Phones away, numbers open. Have your Tracker and bank apps visible.
Curiosity over criticism. "Help me understand" works better than "Why did you."
End with a win. Even if numbers are messy, find one thing you did well.
One conversation at a time. If a hard topic surfaces, use your Money Scripts Cheat Sheet.

Couples Automation Setup GuideThe Couples Wealth Accelerator21
The core idea: Willpower is unreliable. Automation is undefeated. When your money moves before you can spend it, your plan executes itself.
Recommended Account Structures
Option A · Fully Joint
One joint checking
One joint savings
Individual "fun money" accounts
Best for: similar incomes, shared values, low conflict
Option B · Yours/Mine/Ours
Joint checking for shared bills
Individual checking for personal
Joint savings + individual retirement
Best for: different incomes, independence needs
Option C · Proportional
Joint checking (proportional contributions)
Equal personal spending allowances
Shared goals funded jointly
Best for: significant income gap, fairness-focused couples
